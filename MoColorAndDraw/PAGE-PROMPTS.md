# Mo Color & Draw: coloring page prompt pack

**Goal:** about **250 pages** at launch. You have 66; this pack adds **184**: 120 for the existing categories and 64 for 8 new ones.

## How to use it

1. **Start a new ChatGPT chat for each category.** Long chats slowly drift in style.
2. **Attach 1–2 of your existing pages** as the style reference (for example `dolphin` and `rocket`). For **Mo & Friends**, also attach the Mo character image.
3. Paste the **Setup prompt**, wait for "Ready", then paste the page prompts **one at a time**.
4. Download each image and upload it to Drive: `MO COLOR & DRAW / pages / <category folder>`. You don't need to rename anything; I'll check each one, close any gaps in the lines, rename and add it.

**Before uploading, quickly check that:**
- Every line is **closed**, with no gaps where paint could leak out.
- There's **no grey or shading**, only black lines on white.
- There's **no text**, except in the ABC, Hijaiyah and Numbers pages.
- Pages with counting show the **right number** of objects (ChatGPT sometimes miscounts).

## The 3 levels (all for kids 3+, none is hard)

| Level | Age | What it looks like | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EASY** | 3–4 | One big subject, very thick lines, about 6–12 big areas, no background or only a simple ground line | ~75% |
| **MEDIUM** | 4–6 | The subject plus 2–3 simple background items (sun, cloud, tree), about 12–25 areas | ~20% |
| **BIG KID** | 5–7 | A small simple scene, still thick lines and no tiny parts, about 25–40 areas | ~5% |

## Setup prompt (paste first in every new chat)

```
You are making coloring pages for a kids' coloring app (ages 3+). The images I attached show the exact style I want.

STYLE RULES FOR EVERY PAGE
- Landscape 1536 x 1024.
- Pure black outlines on a pure white background. NO grey, NO shading, NO gradients, NO filled black areas (except small eye pupils), NO texture.
- Thick, smooth, even lines, like a children's coloring book.
- EVERY shape must be fully closed, with no gaps between lines, so a paint bucket fill stays inside each area.
- Cute, friendly, rounded cartoon style with happy faces on animals and objects where it fits.
- Keep a white margin around the drawing; nothing touches the edges.
- NO text, letters or numbers, unless I ask for them.
- Islamic-friendly: no pigs, no alcohol, no crosses or other religious symbols, no six-pointed stars, no statues or idols, no music instruments. Never show prophets or companions. Women and girls wear a hijab and modest clothes.

LEVELS (I will write the level in each request)
- EASY (ages 3-4): one big subject filling most of the page, very thick lines, about 6-12 large areas, no background or just a simple ground line.
- MEDIUM (ages 4-6): the subject plus 2-3 simple background items (sun, cloud, tree, ground), about 12-25 areas.
- BIG KID (ages 5-7): a small simple scene, still thick lines and no tiny parts, about 25-40 areas.

Make ONE coloring page per message. Reply "Ready" and wait for the first page.
```

Screenshots

Captured from the real game with Playwright, then framed with a caption. Apple takes up to 10 per language; Google Play takes 2 to 8 phone screenshots (use 01–08). Both sets are JPEG with no transparency.

App Store listing text

Paste each field into App Store Connect. The counter shows Apple's limit (keywords count in bytes, so don't add spaces after commas). Words already in the name and subtitle are left out of the keywords, because Apple indexes those already.

App information

The fixed facts for the New App form and the App Information page.

Age rating

App Information → Age Rating. With these answers the rating comes out as 4+.

App privacy

App Privacy → Data Types. The game makes no network requests at all: no analytics, no ads, no accounts. The privacy label is Data Not Collected.

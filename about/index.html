How to play

Easy to learn. Hard to put down.

1

Find a matching pair

Tap or drag between two identical tiles: apples, kittens, lanterns, planets…

2

Connect with up to two turns

The path can bend twice and travel around the board, but nothing may block it.

3

Collect stars along the way

Every empty cell the path crosses pops a star. Longer clever paths earn more.

4

Clear the board, beat the boss

Ice, stone, gold and bomb tiles, falling boards and shaped puzzles keep every level fresh.

Questions

Good to know.

Can I try it now?

Yes. Nur Connect plays right now in any modern browser on phone, tablet or computer. The App Store and Google Play versions are on the way.

What ages is it for?

Everyone from 3 years up. Little mode (3–5) uses small boards with no timer and a guiding hand. Family mode (13+) has big boards, timers and tricky special tiles.

How are prayer times calculated?

On your device, from the city you pick, using the Kemenag RI method (Fajr 20°, Isha 18°, Asr Shafi'i, +2 minutes ihtiyat). There are 27 cities across Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Brunei, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Türkiye.

Does it collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, analytics or ads. Stars, stickers and settings are stored only on your device, and "Reset progress" in Settings removes them.

Who made Nur Connect?

Mo Games by IBRAZ Group, the team behind Baby Mo. We make playful apps that help Muslim kids build good habits.
